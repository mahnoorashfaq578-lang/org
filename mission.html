A HISTORY WRITTEN
BEYOND EARTH.

Each mission represents another step in Pakistan's development of space science, satellite technology, communication and Earth-observation capabilities.

1962 01
BEGINNING

Rehbar-1

Rehbar-1 marked an important beginning for Pakistan's space and atmospheric research activities. The sounding rocket programme provided an early platform for studying the upper atmosphere and building national scientific capability.

TYPE Sounding Rocket
FIELD Atmospheric Research
1990 02
FIRST SATELLITE

Badr-1

Badr-1 became Pakistan's first experimental digital communications satellite. The mission represented a major transition from sounding rockets toward satellite technology and digital communication experiments.

TYPE Experimental Satellite
FIELD Digital Communication
2001 03
EARTH OBSERVATION

Badr-B

Badr-B continued the development of Pakistan's satellite capabilities with Earth-observation and imaging experiments. It contributed to scientific learning and the development of space-based observation systems.

TYPE Earth Observation
FIELD Imaging & Research
2011 04
COMMUNICATION

PAKSAT-1R

PAKSAT-1R strengthened Pakistan's satellite communication infrastructure. Its services supported applications including broadcasting, telecommunications and connectivity across different regions.

TYPE Communication Satellite
FIELD Broadcasting & Connectivity
2018 05
REMOTE SENSING

PRSS-1

PRSS-1 expanded Pakistan's Earth-observation capabilities. Remote-sensing data can support applications such as agriculture, mapping, environmental monitoring and disaster management.

TYPE Remote Sensing
FIELD Earth Observation
2018 06
TECHNOLOGY

PakTES-1A

PakTES-1A supported Pakistan's development of indigenous satellite technology. The programme provided experience in designing, developing and testing systems intended for future space missions.

TYPE Technology Demonstrator
FIELD Satellite Development
2024 07
MODERN CONNECTIVITY

PakSAT-MM1

PakSAT-MM1 represents the continuing development of Pakistan's communication satellite infrastructure, with a focus on modern connectivity and advanced satellite communication services.

TYPE Communication Satellite
FIELD Broadband Connectivity
2025 08
SATELLITE DEVELOPMENT

PRSC-S1

Pakistan's satellite development efforts continue to expand Earth-observation and space-technology capabilities, building experience for increasingly advanced satellite systems.

TYPE Satellite Mission
FIELD Space Technology

HIGH RESOLUTION

High-resolution Earth-observation technology can provide detailed imagery and information useful for mapping, environmental observation and other applications that require accurate information about the Earth's surface.
